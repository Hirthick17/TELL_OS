Problems we found

What actually breaks every day

Mapped from real conversations with small sellers across India.

01

The 3-sheet disaster

Every product arrival, every order, every return — three sheets to update by hand. One missed entry and all three drift wrong.

02

Stockout blindspot

No alert before your bestseller runs out. You find out only when a customer orders and you can't deliver.

03

Reorder from memory

You buy what you think sold well. Three months later you have dead stock in your room that nobody wants.

04

Orders lost in chats

An order sits inside a thread with 200 messages. You scroll past it. The customer never hears back.

05

Revenue is not profit

Courier, returns, packaging, platform fees — the real number is much lower than what you tell your family.

06

No customer memory

Hundreds of buyers from last year. Zero record of who they are or what they bought. Re-engagement is impossible.

07

Catalog limits and blocks

300-product cap. Uploads fail without reason. Sometimes the whole catalog gets blocked mid-season.

08

Festival season panic

Diwali is 4 weeks away. You don't know what sold last year. You order based on instinct. You guess wrong.

09

No tool reads WhatsApp

Orders, customers, suppliers — all live in WhatsApp. Every other tool asks you to re-enter everything by hand.
